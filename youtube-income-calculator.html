HOW IT WORKS

Where do these numbers come from?

📺

RPM

What a creator earns per 1,000 views, after YouTube takes its share. Estimate = views ÷ 1,000 × RPM. Real RPM varies by niche, viewer country, season and video length.

🔗

Link data

Subscribers, views and video lengths come from the public YouTube Data API. Monthly views are estimated from recent uploads, or the channel's lifetime average if that is higher (for channels whose older videos are still watched). Real revenue and watch hours are only visible to the channel owner.

🇺🇸

US tax

Malaysia has no tax treaty with the US, so Google withholds 30% of earnings from US viewers. Without tax info, up to 24% of all earnings can be withheld.

FAQ

Common questions about YouTube income

How much does YouTube pay per 1,000 views in Malaysia?

For long-form videos with a mostly Malaysian audience, a rough estimate is RM1 to RM15 per 1,000 views, depending on the niche. Entertainment and gaming usually sit at the low end, while finance and tech pay more because advertisers are willing to pay more. Example: 100,000 views on a lifestyle video at RM3 per 1,000 views is about RM300. This figure is called RPM, which is what the creator earns after YouTube takes its share.

How many subscribers do I need to start earning from ads?

To join the YouTube Partner Program and get a share of ad revenue, a channel needs 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days. Until it is accepted, the channel does not earn ad revenue even if ads appear on its videos.

Why do Shorts pay so much less than long-form videos?

Shorts ads run between videos in the feed, not inside your video. The revenue is pooled, split by each creator's share of views, and creators keep 45% of their share. That is why a million Shorts views with a Malaysian audience is estimated at only around RM30 to RM180.

Do Malaysian YouTubers pay US tax?

Yes, on views from the US. Malaysia has no tax treaty with the US, so Google withholds 30% of earnings from US viewers if you have submitted your tax info (the W-8BEN form in AdSense). If you have not, Google may withhold up to 24% of all your earnings, not just the US portion.

Do I need to declare YouTube income to LHDN?

Yes. YouTube income, sponsorships and affiliate earnings count as business income, so declare them in Form B (for individuals with business income). US tax withheld by Google does not replace Malaysian income tax. Keep records of AdSense payments and related expenses such as recording equipment.

Can I find out how much another channel really earns?

No. YouTube does not publish any channel's earnings, so every calculator online, including this one, only gives an estimate based on views. Only the channel owner can see the real figures in YouTube Studio. If you own the channel, enter your actual RPM in the calculator above for a more accurate estimate.

This is an estimate, not real earnings

YouTube does not publish any channel's earnings. The RPM ranges here are Bajet MY's rough estimates based on ranges reported by creators, and can differ a lot from real earnings. Shorts are identified as videos of 3 minutes or less. A channel must be accepted into the YouTube Partner Program before it can earn ad revenue.
